# Changelog

## 0.1.0

* First version. It covers what `plugin-sdk-go` covers: the lifecycle, the
  ten capabilities, the host client, the logger, the gRPC transport (a Unix
  socket, or a loopback port with a bearer token) next to stdio, the `http`
  listener with the per process secret, the `log.sink` stream, the
  standalone JSON-RPC peer and the generated bindings of the contract.
